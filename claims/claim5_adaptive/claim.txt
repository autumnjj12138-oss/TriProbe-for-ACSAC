CLAIM 5 -- The defense holds against adaptive attackers that do not model the
           probe.
Paper: Section 6.4, Tables 11 and 12, plus the adaptive-attacker discussion.

WHAT THE PAPER CLAIMS
Three stronger attackers, none of which reaches the deployment threshold:

  1. Update scaling. The malicious delta is amplified before upload.
  2. Split-trigger. A white-box attacker who knows the Head-Aware routing and
     Layer-1 blocking splits the backdoor so each half is learnable within its
     own subspace, bypassing the architectural constraint.
  3. Delayed and on-off. The backdoor stays dormant and activates late, or
     toggles, so the client can look clean on any given round.

TWO THINGS WORTH KNOWING
Bounded vs unbounded. The submitted implementation followed Bagdasaryan et
al.'s norm-bounded variant: the delta is multiplied by 5 and then clipped to
twice the median benign update norm. The paper described plain 5x scaling. Both
settings are provided here. The stronger, unbounded attack produces a LOWER ASR
under TriProbe (0.92% vs 1.14%), which is consistent with the mechanism:
amplification makes the malicious model more extreme and therefore easier for
an output-space filter to catch. Norm bounding is a way to evade norm-based
filters, and it does not help against this one.

Why the delayed variants stay low. Per-round logs show ASF actually misses
attackers MORE often after a late activation (up to 93% of post-activation
rounds for delay25). The attack nonetheless fails because late activation
leaves too few rounds to implant. The margin here comes from budget, not from
reliable detection, and the paper says so.

HOW IT IS CHECKED
TriProbe below 5% in every configuration (scaling, split-trigger, and at --full
the delayed and on-off arms); the unbounded TriProbe ASR no more than 2 points
above the bounded one; and the undefended scaling arms above 70% (40% at
--quick), and the undefended split-trigger arm above 40% (--full), so that a
run where the attack simply failed to implant is not mistaken for a successful
defense.

This claim is graded SENSITIVE. Our own hardware change moved the scaling
number by roughly 7x. Check the assertions, not the point values.
