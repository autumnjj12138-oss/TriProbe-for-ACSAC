CLAIM 1 -- The composite backdoor defeats published FL backdoor defenses, and
           TriProbe stops it.
Paper: Section 6.2, Tables 2 and 3.

WHAT THE PAPER CLAIMS
An attacker who splits a weak trigger across the header and temporal feature
subspaces evades every defense we tested that reasons about update geometry,
because neither half is anomalous on its own. TriProbe, which instead scores
each client by its target-class response to a synthetic triggered probe,
reduces attack success rate (ASR) to about 1% while the baselines stay near the
undefended level.

REFERENCE VALUES (5 seeds, GTX 1650; see expected/expected.json)
  TriProbe            0.86 +/- 0.68 %      <- below the 5% deployment threshold
  FLAME               57.72 %
  DeepSight           63.08 %
  FedAvg (no defense) 63.13 %
  RLR                 64.44 %
  FedMedian           66.57 %
  Krum                73.29 %
  Lockdown            100.00 %  (benign accuracy also collapses to 80.30%)

Krum's five-seed standard deviation is +/- 20.84, which is why the paper reports
distributions rather than the single-seed numbers of the original submission.

HOW IT IS CHECKED
The assertion is the separation, not the absolute value: every baseline above
40% ASR, TriProbe below 5%, and at least a 20x gap. That margin spans orders of
magnitude and therefore survives the hardware differences described in use.txt.

Lockdown is included as a distinct outcome: it does not merely fail, it
collapses, losing 17 points of benign accuracy. Claim 3 shows why.
